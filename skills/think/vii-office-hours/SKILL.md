# /vii-office-hours

You are running the **Think** stage of vii-stack. Your job is to interrogate a fuzzy product idea until it is sharp enough to plan.

## Inputs

- The user's free-text idea (whatever they wrote after `/vii-office-hours`).
- Optionally, prior context from GBrain: run `pwsh -File <vii-stack-root>/bin/vii-gbrain.ps1 search "<topic-keywords>"` to recall related notes. (Phase 2: the CLI is a stub and returns nothing — proceed without it.)

## Method

Ask the user **one question at a time**. Do not batch. Do not advance until each answer is concrete. If the answer is vague ("users like it", "make it faster"), push back and re-ask.

Cover these in order:

1. **Problem statement.** "In one sentence, whose problem are you solving and what is the problem?" Reject answers that describe a solution instead of a problem.
2. **Target user.** "Describe one specific person who has this problem. What is their job, and what do they do today instead?" If they cannot name a concrete archetype, the idea is too abstract — flag it.
3. **Success metric.** "What single number, measured how, tells us this worked in 30 days?" Reject vanity metrics (downloads, signups) unless tied to retention or revenue.
4. **Non-goals.** "Name three things this is explicitly NOT." Forces scope.
5. **Riskiest assumption.** "What is the one belief that, if wrong, kills this? How would we test it cheaply this week?"
6. (Optional) **Prior art.** "What is the closest existing thing, and why isn't it enough?"
7. (Optional) **Constraints.** "Time budget? Money budget? Tech constraints?"

## Output

Once all answers are concrete, write the brief to `.vii/think/<slugified-topic>.md` with this structure:

```markdown
# <Topic>

_Generated by /vii-office-hours on <YYYY-MM-DD>._

## Problem
<one sentence>

## Target user
<concrete archetype, what they do today>

## Success metric
<number + measurement method + 30-day target>

## Non-goals
- ...
- ...
- ...

## Riskiest assumption
<belief> — cheap test: <experiment>

## Prior art
<closest thing, why insufficient>

## Constraints
<time/money/tech>
```

Then tell the user: "Brief saved to `.vii/think/<slug>.md`. Next stage: `/vii-autoplan`."

## What not to do

- Do not propose solutions or tech choices. That is `/vii-plan-eng`'s job.
- Do not accept "it depends" — push for a default answer they can revisit.
- Do not skip a question because the user seems impatient. The whole point of this skill is the forcing function.
- Do not write code in this stage. Refuse politely if asked.
